# Credit

Work menu → Credit. The desk is a high-yield book. Issuers are keyed by the 10-digit SEC CIK. Paramount Skydance, CIK `0002041610`, is the loaded capital structure of named notes. Every other name opens the annual-report detail from SEC companyfacts: borrowings, the current portion, cash, interest, leases, and the contractual maturity schedule. A named coupon, a rating, and a TRACE price are not in that file and stay not found. The list still filters by sector, search, and a yield floor. The floor starts at 6%. A priced bond under that floor leaves the book. A 3–4% coupon is not this desk. Coupons and clean prices are typed. They are not a live TRACE print. A clean price typed on the Paramount pricing card is kept on that card and stored as a desk entry. The call is still code, with a 40% recovery assumption until a structure is loaded. Yield settlement is the next business day, Saturday and Sunday rolled forward. It is not the October 5 new-issue close. The Treasury par curve and the ICE BofA spread benchmarks load for the latest published day. A failed fetch stays missing. The fixture close is a historical record only. WBD exchange notes keep the year from the 8-K/A name. The month and day were not in that table, so no maturity day is stored and no yield is run off a placeholder.

The page shows the capital structure, three leverage bases, a manual clean price, covenants, a recovery waterfall, three default measures that are not blended, and the invert paths. Code sets the Buy / Watch / Avoid call. A model does not.

Low and unconfirmed facts stay out of the totals. Missing covenants say "not public". Missing prices say "not found". Agency default tables are not loaded.

`GET /api/credit/issuers` reads the local book and does not fetch the network. `GET /api/credit/issuers/{cik}` for a book name other than Paramount loads that issuer's companyfacts file, once per calendar day, and prices any bonds typed on the blotter. Paramount prices saved desk clean prices. That request loads the Treasury par curve and ICE BofA OAS from the daily public files and caches them until the next date. `POST /api/credit/issuers/{cik}/compute` saves typed clean prices and recomputes. Demo accounts cannot POST. The fixture packet is not written into Postgres. Typed prices are desk entries, separate from the fixture.

Deep dive is refused until the model packet is attested. The route will not choose Grok or Claude on its own.
