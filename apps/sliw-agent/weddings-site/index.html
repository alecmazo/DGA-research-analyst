FAQ

We’ve never danced. Is that OK?

Yes — that’s most of Edyta’s wedding clients. She built a career teaching celebrities with no prior experience on Dancing with the Stars.

Where are lessons?

Primarily San Rafael (Marin). Venue or SF sessions available for Dream packages and day-of support.

When should we start?

Ideal: 3–6 months out. The 10-lesson package is designed for that arc. Last-minute polish is possible — book a discovery call.

Can one of us be more advanced?

Absolutely. Lessons are customized so both partners feel confident.
